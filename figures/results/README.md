# Aggregate result figures

These figures are non-identifying visual summaries intended to make the study design and findings easier to understand. They do not show patient-level records.

| Figure group | What it communicates |
|---|---|
| `overall_mae_comparison.png` | Aggregate comparison of the main VF-history model families and baselines. |
| `region_wise_transfer_learning_performance.png` | Transfer-learning performance across the fixed VF regions. |
| `appendix_stage_region_heatmap.png` | Exploratory performance stratification by baseline severity and VF region. |
| `appendix_modality_effect_forest_revised.png` | Aggregate optional-modality effects under the controlled evaluation protocol. |
| `appendix_eye_effect_distributions.png` | Distribution of patient-eye-level effects used as a complementary summary. |
| `appendix_image_data_rigor_audit.png`, `appendix_image_fixed_checkpoint_audit.png` | Supplementary audits of image availability and fixed-checkpoint analyses. |

The figures support interpretation of the thesis results, but they do not provide a substitute for the full restricted-data analysis or a clinical validation study.
